# Demo-first websites for plumbers at $999

A plan an AI agent carries out itself to turn $100 into the most money in
30 days, for the make.fun competition
[the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138](https://make.fun/bounty/the-plan-make-fun-will-carry-20261122-2138).

Day 1: scrape Google Maps for 900 plumbers in a metro with no website and
build each a free demo site with their name on it. Days 2–30: text 30 owners
a day their demo link and sell it for $999 + $99/mo care. $100 covers the
domain, inbox, and SMS. 870 texts at just 2% = 17 sales: $16,983 + $1,683/mo.

The plan works because the owner sees their own business on a real site
before ever paying — the demo does the selling, and the agent does the
repetitive outreach at a scale no human would sustain for $100.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the plan text (UTF-8, 1–280 characters, single block) and
writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
